隱私權政策

生效日期:2026 年 6 月 11 日

Concento(以下稱「本服務」,網址 https://concento.io)重視你的隱私。本政策說明我們收集哪些資料、如何使用,以及你擁有的權利。

1. 我們收集的資料

  • 帳號資料:你註冊時提供的 Email、顯示名稱;若使用 Google 登入,則包含 Google 提供的名稱與頭像。
  • 閱讀資料:你新增的書籍、閱讀狀態與進度、評分、評論、讀書會留言、追蹤關係。
  • 偏好設定:介面語言、隱私開關等,儲存於你的瀏覽器(localStorage)與你的帳號中。

我們不會收集你的真實姓名、地址、電話或任何付款資訊。

2. 資料的儲存與處理

  • 帳號與閱讀資料儲存於 Google Firebase(Authentication 與 Cloud Firestore)。
  • 網站由 GitHub Pages 提供靜態頁面服務。
  • 書籍封面與簡介來自公開資料來源(Google Books、Open Library);部分封面圖片會經由圖片代理服務(images.weserv.nl)載入。

3. 資料的使用方式

  • 提供核心功能:書架管理、社群評分與評論、閱讀相容度計算、動態牆。
  • 我們不出售、不出租你的個人資料,也不用於廣告投放。

4. 公開與私人的界線

  • 你的書庫預設為私人,只有你主動開啟「公開書庫」後,其他使用者才能瀏覽。
  • 你發表的評分、評論與讀書會留言屬於公開內容,所有使用者皆可見;你可以隨時自行刪除。

5. Cookie 與本機儲存

本服務使用瀏覽器 localStorage 儲存語言偏好與登入狀態(由 Firebase 管理),不使用第三方追蹤 Cookie。

6. 你的權利

  • 你可以隨時在服務內檢視、修改或刪除你的書籍、評論與個人資料。
  • 如需刪除整個帳號及所有相關資料,請來信 contact@concento.io,我們會在合理期間內處理。

7. 兒童隱私

本服務並非以未滿 13 歲兒童為對象。若我們發現誤收兒童個資,將儘速刪除。

8. 政策變更

本政策如有重大變更,我們會在網站上公告。繼續使用本服務即視為同意更新後的政策。

9. 聯絡我們

對本政策有任何疑問,請來信:contact@concento.io

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

Concento ("the Service", https://concento.io) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: the email address and display name you provide at sign-up; if you sign in with Google, the name and avatar provided by Google.
  • Reading data: books you add, reading status and progress, ratings, reviews, book-club comments, and follow relationships.
  • Preferences: interface language and privacy switches, stored in your browser (localStorage) and your account.

We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, or any payment information.

2. Where your data lives

  • Account and reading data are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
  • The site is served as static pages by GitHub Pages.
  • Book covers and descriptions come from public sources (Google Books, Open Library); some cover images load through an image proxy (images.weserv.nl).

3. How we use your data

  • To provide core features: shelf management, community ratings and reviews, reading-compatibility scores, and the activity feed.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Public vs. private

  • Your library is private by default; other users can browse it only after you explicitly enable "public library".
  • Your ratings, reviews, and book-club comments are public content visible to all users; you can delete them at any time.

5. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses browser localStorage for language preference and sign-in state (managed by Firebase). We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

6. Your rights

  • You can view, edit, or delete your books, reviews, and profile inside the Service at any time.
  • To delete your entire account and all associated data, email contact@concento.io and we will process the request within a reasonable time.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If we learn we have collected a child's data by mistake, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will announce them on the site. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@concento.io
